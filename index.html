Homemade Gimbap

Hi! I love Korean cuisine and am excited to share the ingredients & measurements that worked for me as a gluten-free and dairy-free girl.

Ingredients

  • Flavored Nori or Seaweed
  • Carrots
  • Tofu, beef, or shrimp
  • Short grain white rice
  • Sesame seeds
  • Gimbap sauce

    Instructions

    1. Cook the protein
    2. Slice the carrots julienne style.
    3. Prepare the sauce, set aside
    4. Add rice, tofu, sesame seeds carrots onto nori and roll up carefully.
    5. Serve with K-sauce and enjoy!
